Nina Andrejevic

Computational Scientist • Argonne National Laboratory

I'm a computational scientist at Argonne National Laboratory working at the intersection of machine learning and materials characterization. My research focuses on developing physics-aware machine learning models to assist the analysis of X-ray scattering and spectroscopic measurements of quantum materials systems. Alongside my research, I'm also enthusiastic about science communication through teaching and scientific data visualization.
